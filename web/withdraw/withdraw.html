Withdraw from Longjing

This page takes a note back out of the Longjing contract with nothing but the chain, an RPC, your note file and the circuit files. It needs no server, no build and no other host, and it works when opened from disk. Your secret key and accumulator opening stay in this page: the proof is made here, and only the proof goes onchain.

Before you start

Reading your note asks the RPC for getNote(commitment), which tells the RPC provider which note this IP address cares about. A browser wallet reads through its own RPC provider, with the same effect. Use a node of your own, or an RPC you reach through Tor, until Ethereum has private reads.

The exit itself is public: initiateWithdrawal names the commitment and the recipient, so anyone can link them to the deposit. Pay to an address with no history if that matters to you.

Filled from the note. Add one if a request's response was lost, since that request may have been served: an exit that claims fewer requests than were served is slashed.

5. Finish the exit

After the 3-day challenge window, anyone can call finalizeWithdrawal, also while the contract is paused. Come back with the commitment, or the note, and the contract address: the exit is read from the contract.
